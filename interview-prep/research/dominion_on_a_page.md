**The company**
- Richmond-based regulated utility: electricity for **3.6 million** homes and businesses in VA, NC and SC; gas for
  500,000 SC customers. [D1]
- Mission: "reliable, affordable, and increasingly clean energy". [D1]
- About 15,200 employees (end-2025); safety is "the highest priority of Dominion Energy's five core values". [D2]
- CEO Robert Blue; CFO Steven Ridge; Edward Baine leads Dominion Energy Virginia. [D2]

**Latest results (Q3 2026, reported 30 Oct)**
- Operating EPS **$1.28**, against $1.06 a year earlier; GAAP $1.30. [D1]
- 2026 operating EPS expected in the **top half of $3.45–$3.69**; long-term guidance reaffirmed (5–7% annual
  operating EPS growth; ~$65B capital 2026–2030). [D1, D2]
- (V5) Quarterly dividend of 66.75 cents declared 27 Oct, payable 20 Dec. [R-092]

**The NextEra merger (pending)**
- Announced 18 May 2026, all-stock: 0.8138 NEE shares per D share; $2.25B proposed bill credits.
- Dual headquarters in Juno Beach and **Richmond**; the Dominion utility names stay.
- Employment commitments to about 15,000 employees. [D3]
- Shareholders approved (Sep). [D5]
- **Virginia SCC:** the companies' rebuttal testimony is due **2 Nov**; evidentiary hearing from **17 Nov**; final
  order due by **11 Jan 2027**. [D5; R-095]
- **South Carolina:** testimony from other parties received (V5, 28 Oct); rebuttal 18 Nov; hearing **8 Dec**; order
  by 29 Jan 2027. **North Carolina:** hearings from 23 Feb 2027. [R-095]
- **Federal (V5):**
  - FERC sent a deficiency letter (23 Sep); the companies responded on 15 Oct. [R-093]
  - The NRC noticed the licence transfer on 27 Aug. [R-094]
  - No federal order yet. Management: "filed and continue to progress". [R-095]
- Expected close: **second half of 2027**. [D5]
- **SCC staff (19 Oct):** deny as proposed unless conditions are met (including an independent DEV board and keeping
  Virginia employee levels for 10 years). [D9]
- **The Governor:** asked for a six-year residential base-rate freeze and larger credits. [D9]

**Demand and data centres**
- "Over 54 GW" of data-centre capacity in contracting stages, up over 6 GW (13%) since end-2025 (V5); "over 12 GW"
  under service agreements. [D5; R-099]
- 11 data centres (744 MW) connected in 2025. [D2]
- All 10 of the DOM zone's summer peak days in 2026. [D5]
- New **GS-5** rate class (≥25 MW) from 1 Jan 2027, with 14-year commitments and minimum demand charges. [D10, D2]

**Rates and affordability**
- Nov 2025 biennial review: +$566M base rates (2026) and +$210M (2027); ROE 9.80%. [D2]
- Typical residential bill: +$11.24 a month (2026). [D10]
- Fuel-cost securitization of about $900M approved (Sep 2026). [D5]
- The IRP projects residential bills up about 2.3% a year. [D5]

**Reliability and grid programmes (the team's world)**
- 3-year SAIDI ex-major events **133 min** (ending 2025), up from **130**, "primarily due to increased storm
  activity". [D2 p. 10]
- 99.98% availability excluding major storms (2025). [D2]
- **Grid Transformation Plan, Phase IIIB (approved Sep 2025):**
  - mainfeeder hardening on 20 more feeders
  - a new outage management system
  - a remote-sensing analytics programme [D2]
- **Grid Resiliency programmes:** Mainfeeder Hardening, Voltage Island Mitigation, Voltage Optimization (uses smart
  meters), Intelligent Grid Devices/FLISR. [D14]
- **How MFH is measured:** feeders chosen on 5-year outage history; −32% outage minutes on feeders hardened ≥12
  months (as of Nov 2023). [D14]
- **Strategic Underground Program** (separate): ~2,700 miles buried 2014–2025 of ~4,000 authorised; completion
  expected 2029. [D2]
- **Why it matters financially:** the SCC may move Dominion's allowed ROE by up to **±50 bp** on factors including
  reliability. [D2]
- **Recent:** salt from an early-October nor'easter caused ~20,000 outages in Hampton Roads. [D11]

**Offshore wind (CVOW)**
- 2.6 GW, 176 turbines planned. [D2]
- About **85% complete**, 44 turbines (over 640 MW) installed (Q3). Sept–Oct installation fell behind on weather and
  vessel maintenance; that maintenance is now done (V5). [D5; R-098]
- Budget **$11.65B**, with $123M of contingency unused; completion now **end-2027** (was "early 2027" in Feb).
  [D5, D2; R-098]
- Stonepeak holds a 50% noncontrolling interest. [D1]

**Three things to remember**
1. Merger decisions land in **January 2027** (VA, SC).
2. **Data-centre growth** is the demand story.
3. **Reliability** is reported to the SCC, can move the allowed return, and Dominion says storms drove the
   130 → 133 rise.
