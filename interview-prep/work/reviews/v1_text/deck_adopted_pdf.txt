Definitions: SAIDI = SAIFI × CAIDI, and major event days are set 
aside by rule
SAIDI
outage minutes per customer 
per year
=
SAIFI
interruptions per customer 
per year: how often
×
CAIDI
minutes per interruption: how 
long
Dominion 2025: 1.320 × 109.4 = 144.4 minutes (excluding major event days)
Major event days (MED): IEEE 1366's 2.5 beta method takes five years of daily SAIDI, drops zero days, and sets α and β as 
the mean and standard deviation of ln(daily SAIDI); a day above exp(α + 2.5β) is an MED.
Dominion reports to EIA under a non-IEEE rule (“Other” in EIA-861); most peers in this deck use IEEE 1366.
With major event days, 2025 SAIDI was 306.7 minutes, against 144.4 without them: MEDs held 53%.
Log split: ln(SAIDI ratio) = ln(SAIFI ratio) + ln(CAIDI ratio). From 2022, duration fell, so frequency accounts for more than all 
of the rise; from 2023, 79%.
Source: IEEE Std 1366; EIA-861 reliability, Dominion Energy Virginia, 2022, 2023 and 2025.
