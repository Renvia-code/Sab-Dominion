Body text at 10.5 pt

Dominion reported that its rolling three-year average SAIDI rose from about 130 minutes (2022–2024) to 133 minutes (2023–2025), excluding major event days. These are overlapping rolling averages, not two separate periods. The public EIA-861 data shows the same direction and lets you split the change into frequency and duration: SAIDI = SAIFI × CAIDI, so ln(SAIDI ratio) = ln(SAIFI ratio) + ln(CAIDI ratio).
