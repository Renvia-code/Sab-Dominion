RISKS

  • Blue stated, "September and October were below due to a combination of weather and Charybdis maintenance activities," regarding the cadence of turbine installations at the Coastal Virginia Offshore Wind project.

SUMMARY

Dominion Energy, Inc. (D +2.30%) reported third-quarter results and reaffirmed its long-term financial guidance while raising the expected full-year outcome to the top half of its original range. Management reported significant progress on its Coastal Virginia Offshore Wind project and emphasized the durable demand from data center customers in Virginia. The company is actively navigating the regulatory approval process for its proposed combination with NextEra Energy, with evidentiary hearings scheduled to begin in late 2026 and early 2027. The company continues to execute its financing plan, which includes the approval of a fuel securitization petition to manage customer bill impacts.

  • The proposed merger with NextEra Energy is expected to close in the second half of 2027, according to Blue.
  • Turbine installation efficiency at CVOW has improved, with the most recent nine turbines averaging 38 hours per installation compared to 69 hours for the first 10.
  • Management reported that 10 of the Dom Zone's summer peak demand days occurred in 2026, with DESC setting a new all-time electric peak in August.
  • The company utilized a portion of its schedule contingency for CVOW due to weather and maintenance but continues to target project completion by the end of 2027.
  • Ridge stated, "We now have over 54 gigawatts of data center capacity in various stages of contracting," highlighting a 13% increase in contracts since the previous year.
  • A final order regarding the NextEra combination in Virginia is expected from the State Corporation Commission by Jan. 11, 2027.

INDUSTRY GLOSSARY

  • 45Z Credits: Federal tax credits for the production of clean transportation fuels, including renewable natural gas.
  • CVOW: Coastal Virginia Offshore Wind, a 2.6-gigawatt offshore wind project being developed by Dominion Energy.
  • DESC: Dominion Energy South Carolina.
  • DEV: Dominion Energy Virginia.
  • IRP: Integrated Resource Plan, a document outlining how a utility plans to meet projected customer demand over a multiyear period.
  • LCOE: Levelized Cost of Energy, the average net present cost of electricity generation for a generating plant over its lifetime.
  • PPA: Power Purchase Agreement, a long-term contract between an electricity generator and a customer.
  • REC: Renewable Energy Certificate, a market-based instrument that represents the property rights to the environmental attributes of renewable electricity generation.
