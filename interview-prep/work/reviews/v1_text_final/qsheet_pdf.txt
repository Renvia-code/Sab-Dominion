10 What are star and snowflake schemas? Fact vs dimension
tables?
Facts hold measurements at one grain; dimensions describe
them. A star joins each dimension directly to the fact, many-to-
one; a snowflake normalizes dimensions into more tables. Power
BI works best with a star. In the review: The core is a small star:
one fact table at utility × state × year, many-to-one to a utility
dimension, with customer-weighted measures. The other tables
are pre-built summaries for particular pages, and most stand
alone. In production I'd conform them to shared date and county
dimensions.

12 What does CALCULATE do? Explain filter context.
CALCULATE evaluates an expression under modified filters.
Filter context is every filter reaching a value: slicers, rows and
columns, page and visual filters. CALCULATE adds, overrides or
removes filters, and turns row context into filter context. In the
review the KPI cards use Selected SAIDI = VAR SelectedYear =
SELECTEDVALUE('Dashboard Year'[Year], 2025) RETURN
CALCULATE([SAIDI ex MED], FCT_RELIABILITY[YEAR] =
SelectedYear), on a disconnected year table: the slicer sets the
cards' year without filtering the page.

Utility domain
15 What are SAIDI, SAIFI and CAIDI, and how are they related?
(Could you calculate them?)
SAIDI = SAIFI × CAIDI: how often, times how long. SAIFI =
customer interruptions ÷ customers served. SAIDI = customer-
minutes of interruption ÷ customers served. CAIDI = SAIDI ÷
SAIFI, minutes per interruption. Dominion 2025, excluding major
events: 1.320 × 109.4 = 144.4 minutes. Sum minutes and
customers before dividing.
16 SAIFI is rising. How would you investigate it?
Rule out measurement first, then follow interruptions to causes.
Check definitions, the major-event rule and any detection change
(smart meters). Split by cause code, feeder and device, weather
exposure and repeat customers (CEMIn). From 2022 to 2025 SAIFI
rose 10%, CAIDI fell 4%, and storm county-days rose about 10%:
consistent with Dominion's “primarily due to increased storm
activity”. Confirming a cause needs outage records.
17 What is a major event day, and why exclude it?
A day extreme enough to swamp the trend, set aside by rule.
IEEE 1366's 2.5β method: from five years of daily SAIDI, a day is a
major event day if its SAIDI exceeds exp(α + 2.5β), α and β being
the mean and standard deviation of ln(daily SAIDI). Excluding
them shows day-to-day performance. What it hides: in 2025, 53%
of Dominion's all-event minutes (162.3 of 306.7). Dominion uses
its own method (“Other”): peer ranks are indicative.
