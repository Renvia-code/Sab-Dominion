10What is a star schema (vs a snowflake schema)? Fact vs dimension tables?

Facts hold measurements at one grain; dimensions describe them. Here the fact is one row per utility, state and year, with customers, customer-minutes and interruptions; dimensions hold descriptions such as utility name and peer group. A star joins each dimension directly to the fact, many-to-one; a snowflake normalises dimensions into more tables. Power BI works best with a star: FCT_RELIABILITY → DIM_UTILITY in the review.

12What does CALCULATE do? Explain filter context.

CALCULATE evaluates an expression under modified filters. Filter context is every filter reaching a value: slicers, rows and columns, page and visual filters. CALCULATE adds, overrides or removes filters, and turns row context into filter context. In the review the KPI cards use CALCULATE([SAIDI ex MED], FCT_RELIABILITY[YEAR] = SELECTEDVALUE('Dashboard Year'[Year], 2025)) on a disconnected year table: the slicer sets the cards' year without filtering the page.

Utility domain

15What are SAIDI, SAIFI and CAIDI, and how are they related?

SAIDI = SAIFI × CAIDI: how often, times how long. SAIFI = customer interruptions ÷ customers served. SAIDI = customer-minutes of interruption ÷ customers served. CAIDI = SAIDI ÷ SAIFI, minutes per interruption. Dominion 2025, excluding major events: 1.320 × 109.4 = 144.4 minutes. Sum minutes and customers before dividing.

16What is a major event day, and why exclude it?

A day extreme enough to swamp the trend, set aside by rule. IEEE 1366's 2.5-beta method: from five years of daily SAIDI, a day is a major event day when its SAIDI exceeds exp(α + 2.5β), α and β being the mean and standard deviation of ln(daily SAIDI). Excluding them shows day-to-day performance. What it hides: in 2025 they were 53% of Dominion's all-event minutes (162.3 of 306.7). Dominion reports under its own rule.

17SAIFI is rising. How would you investigate it?

Rule out measurement first, then follow interruptions to causes. Check definitions, the major-event rule and whether detection changed (smart meters). Then split by cause code, feeder and device, weather exposure and repeat customers (CEMI). From 2022 to 2025, SAIFI rose 10% while CAIDI fell 4%, and storm county-days rose from 718 to 791: consistent with Dominion's “primarily due to increased storm activity”. Confirming a cause needs outage records.
